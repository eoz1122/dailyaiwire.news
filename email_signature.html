📧 Email Signature Setup Instructions

  1. Select the signature below (click and drag to highlight everything from the line to the end)
  2. Copy it (Cmd+C / Ctrl+C)
  3. Open your email client settings:
    • Gmail: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature
    • Outlook: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature
    • Apple Mail: Mail → Settings → Signatures
  4. Paste the signature into the signature field
  5. Customize the name and title as needed

💡 Pro Tip: For best results in Apple Mail, paste into TextEdit first, then copy from TextEdit into Mail to preserve formatting.
