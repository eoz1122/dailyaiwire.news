# Daily AI Wire — Project-Specific Rules

> These rules supplement the Universal Directives in `~/.gemini/GEMINI.md` (v3.0).

## 1. Free-Forever Policy

Daily AI Wire is a **free, open-access** platform. There will be **no paywalls, premium tiers, or gated content** — ever.

- All features, including AI-powered tools (search, research assistant), are available to all users at no cost.
- Revenue comes from partnerships and sponsorships, **never** from user subscriptions or premium gates.
- No feature should be labeled or designed as "premium" or "paid."

## 2. Brand Voice

- **Hybrid Intelligence**: AI-scale production + human editorial judgment
- **Tone**: Professional, direct, investment-grade analysis
- **No banner ads**: Revenue through value-aligned partnerships only

## 3. Editorial Standards

- All articles must include structured analysis: gist, why_it_matters, bull_case, bear_case
- Kill decisions route to the Iron Judo lead pipeline automatically
- Background music uses `Background Mucis.mp3` and `ES_Cinematics - Blue Saga` (do not delete)
